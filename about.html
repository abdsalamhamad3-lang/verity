Verity Codeworks Limited is a UK-incorporated software business creating practical technology for productivity, wellbeing, project delivery and access to skilled services.

Our story

Clarity first. Useful technology always.

We believe thoughtful software can make complicated days easier to navigate, healthy habits more sustainable and team delivery more transparent.

Our products span personal effectiveness, fitness planning, project coordination and ProWorks, an on-demand tradespeople platform currently serving Nigeria.

We build with an emphasis on straightforward experiences, privacy-aware decisions and the ability to evolve with customer needs.
